###  Pharmacy Sales Dashboard – Product & Regional Performance Analysis
# 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes pharmacy sales data across product categories, years, and states to identify patterns, high-performing products, and areas of underperformance. The goal is to provide actionable insights that can help pharmacy managers and stakeholders optimize sales strategies, improve inventory planning, and strengthen decision-making.

# Problem Statement:
Pharmacy sales data was available but fragmented, making it difficult to clearly identify product categories driving growth, underperforming drugs, and regional differences. Without a consolidated dashboard, decision-makers lacked the visibility needed for evidence-based resource allocation.

# Business Impact:
This analysis equips decision-makers with actionable insights for targeted marketing, better inventory control, and informed sales strategies — ultimately improving pharmacy revenue and efficiency.

# 2. Objectives

Analyze total pharmacy sales across multiple years (2020–2024).

Identify top-performing and underperforming product categories.

Track year-over-year sales trends to detect growth or decline.

Map sales distribution by states to identify high- and low-performing regions.

Provide actionable recommendations for product promotion, inventory management, and regional sales strategies.

# 3. Data Sources

The analysis utilized structured pharmacy sales datasets with the following attributes:

Year: Sales year for trend analysis.

Product Category: Classification of drugs (e.g., Painkillers, Antidiabetics, etc.).

Total Sales: Volume of drugs sold per product.

Average Sales per Product: Benchmark for evaluating performance.

Geographic Data: State-level distribution for mapping.

Tools Used: Tableau (dashboard creation & visualization), Excel (data preprocessing).

# 4. Data Cleaning and Preparation

Standardize product category names for consistency.

Verified totals for each year and removed duplicate entries.

Structured data for year-over-year and state-level comparisons.

Calculated average sales per product as a benchmark to evaluate categories.

# 5. Methodology

Descriptive Analysis: Total sales, average sales per product, and state-level performance.

Trend Analysis: Sales volume tracked over time (2020–2024).

Product Category Analysis: Ranking by sales and contribution to total revenue.

Geospatial Analysis: Mapping sales by state to identify regional strengths and gaps.

Benchmarking: Compared categories against average sales per product to identify above/below average performers.

# 6. Key Findings
6.1 Total Sales

Total Sales (2020–2024): 3,628,500 units

Average Sales per Product: 18,704 units

Sales remained relatively stable across years with minor fluctuations.

6.2 Top Performing Categories

Painkillers: Highest sales volume (~850K units), consistently outperforming average.

Antidiabetics & Antihypertensives: Strong contributors, reflecting chronic disease burden.

6.3 Underperforming Categories

IV Fluids & Anticonvulsants: Below average sales; limited contribution compared to top categories.

6.4 Regional Sales (State-Level)

Wide variation across states; some regions recorded as high as ~168K units while others lagged at ~124K.

Highlights opportunities for targeted regional marketing and distribution improvements.

6.5 Sales Trends by Year

Sales showed gradual year-to-year fluctuations but remained stable overall.

Growth aligned more with chronic disease categories rather than acute treatments.

# 7. Insights and Recommendations

Promote High-Demand Categories: Painkillers, Antidiabetics, and Antihypertensives should be prioritized in promotions and inventory planning.

Address Underperforming Products: Investigate low sales in IV Fluids and Anticonvulsants to determine whether it is demand-driven or due to distribution challenges.

Regional Targeting: Strengthen sales operations in underperforming states to balance growth across markets.

Strategic Inventory Management: Allocate resources to high-volume categories while reducing excess stock of low-demand products.

# 8. Conclusion

The Pharmacy Sales Dashboard highlights both opportunities and challenges in pharmacy product performance:

Chronic disease drugs (Painkillers, Antidiabetics, Antihypertensives) drive the majority of sales.

Some regions underperform, signaling untapped potential.

Stable sales trends suggest consistent demand, but underperforming categories may need reassessment.
